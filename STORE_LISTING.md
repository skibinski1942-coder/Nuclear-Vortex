Meet Achilles — the intelligent AI assistant that puts the power of advanced AI research, task automation, and natural conversation in your pocket.

Talk to Achilles in plain language. Ask questions, get answers, delegate tasks, and have natural multi-turn conversations powered by a state-of-the-art AI backend.

Create and run automated workflows. Set triggers by schedule or event, chain actions together, and let Achilles handle repetitive digital work so you don't have to.

Create tasks, set priorities, chain dependencies, and let Achilles track progress. The assistant learns from your patterns to prioritize what matters most.

★ PRIVACY-FIRST DESIGN
No ads. No tracking. No data sold. Achilles connects to your own self-hosted backend, so your conversations stay yours. All communication is encrypted over HTTPS.

Achilles is part of the Nuclear-Vortex family — a technology holding company dedicated to making technology simpler, not harder.

## Pre-Launch Checklist

Before submitting for review, make sure you have:

- [ ] Signed release APK or AAB generated (`android/` → Build → Generate Signed Bundle/APK)
- [ ] Play Developer account created and $25 registration fee paid
- [ ] Payment profile set up (under Payments → Profile in Play Console)
- [ ] Privacy Policy hosted at a public URL (paste URL into Play Console)
- [ ] At least 2 phone screenshots uploaded
- [ ] Hi-res 512 × 512 icon uploaded
- [ ] Feature graphic (1024 × 500) uploaded
- [ ] Content rating questionnaire completed
- [ ] App category set to Productivity
- [ ] "Contains ads" set to No
- [ ] Release notes written in "What's New"
- [ ] App reviewed and approved by Google (typically 1–3 business days for first release)
